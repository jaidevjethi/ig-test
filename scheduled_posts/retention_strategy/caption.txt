Everyone talks about using Instagram to get new patients. Nobody talks about using it to keep the ones you already have.

That is the bigger opportunity.

Acquiring a new patient costs 5x more in time and effort than retaining an existing one. Your existing patients already trust you.

Instagram — used correctly — is the most cost-effective retention tool a clinic has.

Here are 4 ways to turn your followers into returning patients:

📍 The Seasonal Reminder
Post content relevant to conditions your existing patients manage, timed to seasons. Example: "Joint pain patterns I see every December." They think: "My doctor is still thinking about me."

📍 The Follow-Up Carousel
Create content like: "3 Questions to Ask Your Doctor at Your Next Visit — If You Have [Condition]." The patient saves it, feels invested, and returns.

📍 The 'We Remember You' Story
Post a weekly Story acknowledging patients managing specific conditions. It takes 2 minutes but makes them feel cared for.

📍 The Community Post
Turn common patient questions into content. When patients see their worries addressed, they feel ownership. They comment and return.

Retention isn't about reminding patients you exist. It's about making them feel your practice is still working for them between appointments.
